About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Rochester, working with Prof. Zhen Bai in the inter.play Lab. Before that, I got my master's degree from HKUST(GZ), advised by Prof. Mingming Fan. I received my bachelor’s degree in Industrial Design from Tongji University in 2023.

I work on Human-Computer Interaction and Human-AI Collaboration. My research uses interactive technologies such as collaborative XR to support computer-mediated communication and group work. My previous design and engineering projects involve: interaction design, wearable technology, serious games, and AR/VR. Besides research, I enjoy photography, swimming, and playing badminton.

IJHCI In International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction [Paper]

Abstract

Based on interviews with 23 Chinese designers, this study examines how mentoring functions operate in fast-paced design teams. It finds that career support is often oriented toward project efficiency, while psychosocial support is limited by insufficient psychological safety. The paper identifies structural challenges and design opportunities for intelligent systems that reduce mentor workload and relational risk.

Fan Lin, Yiqi Liang, Qianjie Wei, Mingshuo Li, Chutian Jiang, Mingming Fan#.

IMWUT 2026 In Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies [Paper]

Abstract

VR-Shuttlecock combines leg exercises, progressive gameplay, multisensory feedback, and safety measures to support balance training for older adults. In a four-week controlled study, participants using the system showed meaningful balance improvements and positive attitudes toward continued training, suggesting that VR shuttlecock can provide an engaging and practical exercise experience.

Qianjie Wei, Xiaoying Wei, Yiqi Liang, Fan Lin, Nuonan Si, Mingming Fan#.

CHI 2025 In Proceedings of the CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems [Paper]

Abstract

RemoteChess is a multi-user VR community built around the culturally familiar activity of Chinese chess. A study with older adults found that familiar environments, culturally relevant social catalysts, and asymmetric player and spectator roles supported social interaction and connectedness. The work concludes with design guidance for culturally grounded social activities in VR.

Qianjie Wei, Jingling Zhang, Pengqi Wang, Xiaofu Jin, Mingming Fan#.

VINCI 2024 In Proceedings of the International Symposium on Visual Information Communication and Interaction [Paper]

Abstract

Augmented Library is an HMD-based AR system designed to renew engagement with physical libraries. It layers digital interactions onto the physical environment to support book discovery, community participation, and diverse user needs. The paper presents the system design and preliminary feedback on its potential to enrich students’ in-person library experiences.
